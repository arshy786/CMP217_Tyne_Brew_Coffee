Our Story

Independent, North East born and brewed.

How It Started

Tyne Brew Coffee began as a tiny market stall on the Newcastle Quayside back in 2018. Armed with a second-hand machine and a stubborn love of proper coffee, we poured flat whites in all weathers and got to know our regulars by name.

By 2020 we’d outgrown the gazebo and opened our first bricks-and-mortar spot. We kept the simple promise: fair-trade beans, friendly faces, and a space you actually want to sit in.

Meet the Founder

Amina Khan grew up in Heaton and learnt to pull espresso in a tiny café off Chillingham Road. She still roasts test batches every week and insists brownies are a food group.
