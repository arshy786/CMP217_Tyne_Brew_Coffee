Our Signature Favourites

Dark Roast Coffee

Boldest blend with earthy undertones and smoky finish. For lovers of strong coffee with character.

Espresso

Italian-style double shot with rich crema and robust flavour. Ideal for purists and flat white fans.

Medium Roast

Nutty aroma with hints of caramel. Perfectly balanced for lattes or morning filter brews.

Decaf Coffee

CO₂ decaffeinated without compromising body or flavour. Smooth and satisfying, minus the buzz.

Yorkshire Tea (Original)

British classic. Strong and reliable. Blended to pair perfectly with milk or a Ringtons biscuit.

Yorkshire Tea (Decaf)

All the flavour, none of the caffeine. Great for evenings or caffeine-conscious mornings.

Zuma Hot Chocolate

Velvety dark, milk, or white blends. Steamed with oat, soya, or dairy milk to your liking.
